Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Ripe ("we," "us," or "our") builds a food inventory and meal planning app. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. We wrote it to match what the app actually does, not as a generic template.

Information we collect

We collect only what's needed to run the app:

  • Name. Used to greet you in the app and identify you to other members of your shared kitchen.
  • Email address. Used to create and log into your account.
  • User ID. An internal identifier tied to your account, used to authenticate you and keep your data associated with the right account.
  • Content you create. This includes recipe ratings and reviews, pantry and inventory entries, grocery lists, and saved recipes.

We do not collect your location, health or fitness data, financial or payment information, contacts, photos, or browsing history.

How we use your information

We use your information solely to operate the app. That means authenticating your account, syncing your pantry and lists across devices, displaying your name and activity to members of your shared kitchen, and showing ratings and reviews to other users browsing a recipe.

We do not use your information for advertising, and we do not sell or share it with data brokers or advertising networks.

Shared kitchens

Ripe lets you share a pantry with other people using a kitchen code. If you join or create a shared kitchen, your name and activity within that kitchen (items added, lists, saved recipes) become visible to other members of that same kitchen. This is a core part of how the feature works, and it's visible only to people you've shared your code with, not to the public or to other Ripe users outside your kitchen.

How we store your information

Your data is stored using Supabase, our backend infrastructure provider, with industry-standard security practices. We don't operate our own servers for this data; Supabase hosts and secures it on our behalf under their own security practices.

Sharing your information

We don't sell your personal information. We share it only with the service providers that make the app work, currently Supabase for data storage, and with Apple or Google as required to distribute the app through their app stores. We do not share your information with third-party advertisers or data brokers.

Data retention

We keep your information for as long as your account is active. If you'd like your account and associated data deleted, contact us and we'll take care of it.

Your choices

You can access, correct, or delete your information at any time by using the app directly, or by emailing us and we'll handle it for you.

Children's privacy

Ripe isn't directed at children, and we don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we'll delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we make meaningful changes to how we handle your information, we'll update this page and adjust the date at the top.

Contact us

Questions about this policy or your data? Reach us at hello@ripeapp.ca.
